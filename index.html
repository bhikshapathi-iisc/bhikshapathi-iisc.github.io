The Making of an LLM
Completed 2025
LLMTransformerPre-trainingPyTorch
Flash Attention LLaMA Arch GPU Optimized
A full decoder-only Transformer built from first principles — tokenization, scaled dot-product attention, positional encodings, and the complete pre-training loop. Integrates modern SOTA components: Flash Attention, SwiGLU, RMSNorm, and Grouped Query Attention for efficient, reproducible LLM training.
